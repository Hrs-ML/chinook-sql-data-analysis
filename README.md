# Chinook SQL Data Analysis 🎵

SQL-based analysis of the **Chinook database** using PostgreSQL to understand sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, revenue patterns, and employee performance.

## 🎯 Business Objective

The main goal of this project is to turn transactional data into useful business insights using SQL.

The analysis looks at questions such as:

- Which genres, artists, albums, and media types generate the most revenue?
- Which customers contribute the most revenue?
- Which countries perform best in terms of customers and revenue?
- Which tracks and genres are most popular based on sales quantity?
- How does revenue change over time?
- Which employees manage the most customers and generate the most customer revenue?
- Which customers can be considered high-value based on their spending?

The results can help a business understand **where revenue comes from, who its most valuable customers are, which products perform best, and where sales patterns may require attention.**

## 🗂️ Database

The project uses the **Chinook database**, a relational database containing information about:

- Customers
- Invoices and invoice line items
- Tracks
- Albums
- Artists
- Genres
- Media types
- Employees

The relationships between these tables allow sales and customer information to be combined for deeper analysis.

## 📊 Key Business Insights

### Revenue
**Rock** is the strongest revenue-generating genre in the analysis with **$826.65**, substantially ahead of Latin at **$382.14**.

### Artist Performance
**Iron Maiden** is the highest-revenue artist among the results, generating **$138.60**, followed by U2 with **$105.93**.

### Country Performance
The **USA** leads the country analysis with **13 customers and $523.06 in revenue**, while Canada follows with 8 customers and $303.96.

### Customer Value
**Helena Holý** is the highest-spending customer in the top-customer analysis with **$49.62** in total spending.

### Product Performance
The highest-revenue album in the results is **Battlestar Galactica (Classic), Season...** with **$35.82**.

The top tracks are tied at **2 units sold each**, so there is no single track that clearly dominates the dataset based on quantity sold.

### Media Type
**MPEG audio file** generates the majority of revenue among media types, with **$1,956.24**, considerably higher than the other media formats.

### Genre Popularity
**Rock** is also the most popular genre by quantity sold, with **835 tracks sold**, followed by Latin with 386.

### Revenue Trend
Monthly revenue shows fluctuations over time, with several noticeable peaks and dips rather than a consistent upward or downward trend.

### Employee Performance
Among the support employees, **Jane Peacock** manages the most customers (**21**) and has the highest customer revenue (**$833.04**) in the analysis.

### Customer Retention Opportunity
The query for customers with no purchases returned **no records**, indicating that every customer in this dataset has made at least one purchase.
